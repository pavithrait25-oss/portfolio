Sri Homemade Cakes — Online Bakery & Direct WhatsApp Ordering

A modern, attractive, mobile-responsive commercial website developed for Sri Homemade Cakes, a boutique home bakery business in India. Designed to showcase handcrafted cakes, provide transparent pricing, and capture direct orders through an automated WhatsApp commerce workflow.

Key Implementation Highlights:

  • Interactive Cake Menu: Filterable catalog of 10+ artisan cakes (Dutch Truffle, Black Forest, Red Velvet, Cheesecakes) with dietary tags (Egg / Pure Eggless).
  • Dynamic Weight & Price Calculator: Real-time price calculation in INR (₹) based on user-selected cake weights (0.5 Kg, 1.0 Kg, 2.0 Kg).
  • Instant WhatsApp Checkout: Generates a pre-formatted, detailed WhatsApp message with customer name, phone, chosen cake, weight, price, and delivery date with zero friction.
  • Interactive 3D Showcase: Integrated 3D cake showcase with interactive 360-degree rotation and smooth rendering.
  • Mobile-First UX: Slide-out navigation drawer and sticky quick-action bar for rapid ordering on mobile devices.
